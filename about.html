Our Story

Built by a student,
for students.

PGCETHub started because finding PGCET previous year papers was unnecessarily hard. We fixed that — one branch at a time.

Our Mission

Every PGCET student deserves free access to study material

When I was preparing for PGCET, I spent days trying to find previous year question papers online. Most sites were cluttered with ads, broken links, or paywalled content. The good papers were buried in library shelves with no digital access.

So I went to the library, collected every paper I could find, scanned them, and organized them by branch and year. That collection became PGCETHub.

Our mission is simple — make every PGCET question paper freely available to every student in Karnataka, regardless of which college they attend or how much money they have.

How PGCETHub grew

From a folder of scanned PDFs to a full website

Step 1

The problem

Spent days searching for PGCET CS papers online. Found nothing useful — only broken links and spam sites.

Step 2

Library visit

Visited the college library and collected physical copies of papers from 2018 onwards across multiple branches.

Step 3

Scanning & organizing

Scanned every paper using CamScanner, converted to PDF, and organized them branch-wise and year-wise.

Step 4

PGCETHub launches

Built this website so every student in Karnataka can access these papers for free — anytime, anywhere.

Next

Growing with you

Adding more branches, answer keys, topic-wise analysis and solutions — with your support and feedback.
